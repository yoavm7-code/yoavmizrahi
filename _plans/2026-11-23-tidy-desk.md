# The desk arranges itself

Approved 2026-11-23.

## Why

The home desk used hand-typed x/y positions on a fixed 1420px canvas. On a wide screen the right half sat empty, on a laptop the bottom row was cut off, objects overlapped (Memesh under Ad Intel, I.M Engineering over the hint line), and every new work had to be placed by hand. On phones the four tapes were squeezed until their labels read "T…", and nothing could be dragged.

## Goals

- The desk looks composed at every width, from 390px to 2560px, with no overlaps and nothing cut off.
- A new work, film or tape finds its own place. Nobody arranges anything by hand.
- Visitors can drag anything anywhere, on computers and on phones.
- One "Tidy up" button puts everything back.
- Every visit starts with the tidy desk (nothing is remembered).

## The approach

- **Zones, in the admin's order.** The sticky note, the film corner (the monitor with its tapes stacked beside it like a pile of cassettes) and the works, in the order set in the admin.
- **A packer places them** (`js/desk-layout.js`, pure). It measures each object, then places each one in turn at the highest free spot with even gaps (a skyline packer). The result is centered and fills the width of the screen. It runs again on resize.
- **Tilt and a little looseness come from each object's name** (a stable hash: up to ±3° and a few px), so the desk feels hand-placed but looks the same on every visit.
- **Dragging** moves an object by an offset on top of its home spot, so tidying just sets the offsets back to zero, with an animation. It works the same on the desk and on the phone pile. An object can't be dragged off the desk.
  - Mouse: press and drag, as before. Buttons and links inside objects still click.
  - Touch: press and hold (about 0.35s) to lift, then drag. A quick swipe still scrolls the page.
- **"Tidy up"** shows only after something has moved, fixed at the bottom right of the desk.
- **Up to 900px wide** the desk stays a pile you scroll through, but the tapes become a neat 2 by 2 rack under the monitor with readable labels, and objects can be lifted and dragged too.

## What goes away

- Positions and tilt per object (`x`, `y`, `r` in `content/site.json`), the admin's "Arrange the desk" mode, and the page zoom between 900px and 1420px. They're replaced by the packer.
- The admin's "Order on phones" becomes **"Order on the desk"** (`desk.order`, read from the old `desk.mobileOrder` too), used at every width.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Fix the hand layout** (fill the width, auto-place new items): cheapest, but still one hand-made layout for every screen size, and it gets crowded again with every work.
- **Desk plus drawer** (four featured works, the rest in a drawer): the most creative, but older works take an extra click. It can be added on top of the packer later if the desk ever gets too full.

## Architecture

- `js/desk-layout.js`: the one place where desk positions are worked out (pure, no DOM). Loaded before `js/main.js`.
- `js/main.js`: measures the objects, applies the layout, and handles dragging, tidying and the tapes.
- `js/render.js`: renders objects in the desk order; no positions.
- `js/schema.js`: `desk.order` and `deskKeys()`; no positions.
- Guard: `tests/unit/desk-layout.test.mjs` fails if two objects overlap or anything leaves the desk, at many widths and item counts.

## Security

No new data, inputs or network calls. Nothing is stored on the visitor's device.

## Observability

`[desk layout]` logs the width, item count and resulting height on each layout. `[desk drag]` logs the object key and the drop (and a tape dropped on the monitor). `[desk tidy]` logs how many objects were moved back.

## Settings

The order of the desk stays in the admin (one list for every screen size). Tilt, gaps and the hold delay are not exposed: they're craft details, and a knob for them would only let the desk get messy again.

## Testing

- Unit: the packer (no overlaps, nothing off the desk, order kept, centered, same result every time, 1 to 20 works, 900 to 2560px, tapes stack and wrap into columns), and the schema migration from `mobileOrder` and the removed positions.
- Browser: no overlaps at 1180 and 1440, nothing past the right edge at 2560, drag and tidy, a tape dropped on the monitor plays, a press-and-hold drag on a phone, the tape rack readable at 390px.
- Screenshots at 390, 820, 1180, 1440 and 2560 checked by eye.

## Deploy

Branch `tidy-desk`, one PR into `main`. Checks run on the PR. Merging deploys through `deploy.yml`. Rollback: revert the PR, or restore the previous version from the admin's History.
