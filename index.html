01 · PRODUCTION TOOL

Launch Desk

YEAR2026
MADE WITHAI coding agents

Launches ad campaigns in bulk. Fill a sheet with dozens of campaigns, press Launch, and watch each one get built and uploaded, with a plain reason when one fails. It runs in production.

THE REAL THING · FULLY CLICKABLE
LAUNCH DESK · MADE-UP DATA
⤢ FULL SCREEN

The clickable demo is built for a computer screen. Open it anyway →

DESIGN

Made for people who launch dozens of campaigns at once, so every batch is a spreadsheet. Each row is a campaign, each column a setting like country, vertical or landing page, and every row gets a check or a cross once the launch is over.

Cool sage greys under a dark top bar, with one deep green for actions. Red and amber only report what went wrong in a launch. Campaign names and the launch log are set in IBM Plex Mono, so long names line up and are easy to scan. There is a dark mode too.

Toto Mundial

YEAR2026
MADE WITHAI coding agents

A World Cup 2026 prediction pool for friends. Everyone picks the scores before kickoff, and the table settles the arguments.

THE REAL THING · FULLY CLICKABLE
TOTO MUNDIAL · MADE-UP DATA
⤢ FULL SCREEN
DESIGN

Warm and a little festive, like a stadium at sunset: a cream background, terracotta for actions, and a trophy rising over the hills at the top of the home page.

Built for a phone held in one hand. The tabs sit at the bottom, and a pick is two steppers that save as you tap, with an undo for slips. Rank and points are big numbers in Space Grotesk, readable at a glance.

It works in English and in Hebrew. One tap on the globe flips the whole layout to right to left.

DESIGN

Soft and friendly, since the people using it are parents and the staff of a kids' playground. Peach for actions, green for open spots, and rounded cards everywhere.

Three apps share one look. Parents book from their phone. The front desk scans and checks in. The manager gets today's sessions, the day in numbers, and the week ahead shaded by how full each slot is.

Hebrew first and right to left, like the real product, with English one tap away.

04 · WEBSITE

LoreWire

YEAR2026
MADE WITHAI coding agents

A website that turns everyday internet dilemmas into short, hand-drawn stories. Each one ends with a question, and readers vote on it.

VISIT THE LIVE PRODUCT ↗
THE REAL THING · FULLY CLICKABLE
LOREWIRE · LOREWIRE'S OWN STORIES
⤢ FULL SCREEN

The clickable demo is built for a computer screen. Open it anyway →

DESIGN

Dark by default and built for phones first, since the stories are made for phones and social feeds. One red does all the pointing: the active tab, the poll, the progress bar, the read-along highlight.

Four typefaces, each with one job. Fraunces for titles, Archivo for the logo and buttons, Hanken Grotesk for reading, and a mono for labels and durations.

The home page works like a streaming service: a rotating hero, rows of posters and a Top 10. Each poster has its title painted into the art, so a row reads like a shelf of comic covers.

Ad Radar

YEAR2026
MADE WITHAI coding agents

Keeps an eye on the ads competitors run on Facebook and Instagram. Every scan saves the new ones with the page they lead to, and the gap finder shows which topics we haven't written about yet.

THE REAL THING · FULLY CLICKABLE
AD RADAR · MADE-UP DATA
⤢ FULL SCREEN

The clickable demo is built for a computer screen. Open it anyway →

DESIGN

Made for a small team that opens it every morning. Ads is the main list, with the filters beside it. One ad opens in full: the creative, the page it leads to, our own versions of it and a short workflow, and J and K step through the list without going back.

A light, quiet screen: white cards on warm grey with soft corners, set in Plus Jakarta Sans. Numbers, domains and links are in a mono so they line up. One magenta marks what needs a decision, like a gap or the Draft article button. Green and red only show state.

06 · WEBSITE

I.M Engineering

YEAR2026
MADE WITHAI coding agents

The website of I.M Engineering & Construction, a contractor that builds offices, restaurants and shops. It shows the finished projects and turns visitors into calls and messages. In Hebrew, right to left, and made for phones too.

VISIT THE LIVE PRODUCT ↗
THE REAL THING · FULLY CLICKABLE
I.M ENGINEERING · THE LIVE SITE'S CONTENT
⤢ FULL SCREEN
DESIGN

Black, white and one gold, taken from the logo. The photos do the selling, so every project is a full-bleed photo with its category in a small gold pill and the title over a dark fade.

On a computer the menu is a thin rail of icons on the right. On a phone it turns into a tab bar at the bottom, and a call button appears once you scroll past the opening video.

An accessibility panel adds text size and twelve options, from high contrast to a reading guide that follows the pointer.
